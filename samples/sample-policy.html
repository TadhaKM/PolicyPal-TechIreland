General Exclusions

Flood Exclusion

We do not cover loss or damage caused by flood, including but not limited to:

  • Rising water levels from rivers, lakes, or seas
  • Surface water runoff
  • Water escaping from natural water courses

Note: Flood cover may be available as an optional endorsement - please contact your broker.

We do not cover:

  • Wear and tear, gradual deterioration - including rot, rust, mould, fungus, or gradual deterioration
  • Mechanical or electrical breakdown - unless caused by an insured event
  • Insects, vermin or pests - damage caused by insects, rodents, or other pests
  • War and terrorism - loss arising from war, invasion, or act of terrorism
  • Nuclear risks - any loss caused by nuclear reaction or radiation
  • Pollution and contamination - unless caused by an insured event
  • Sonic bangs - pressure waves from aircraft travelling at supersonic speeds
  • Confiscation - by customs or government authorities
  • Business use - any loss arising from business activities conducted at the property

General Conditions

Unoccupancy Clause

If Your Home is left unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days:

  • Cover for escape of water, theft, and malicious damage is excluded
  • You must notify us before the period of unoccupancy begins
  • All water supplies must be turned off at the mains

Security Requirements

You must ensure the following security measures are in place:

  • All external doors must be fitted with 5-lever mortice deadlocks or equivalent
  • All accessible windows must be fitted with key-operated locks
  • If specified in your schedule, a burglar alarm must be maintained in working order

Reasonable Care

You must:

  • Take all reasonable precautions to prevent loss, damage, or injury
  • Maintain the buildings and contents in good condition
  • Comply with all statutory requirements and regulations

How to Make a Claim

In the event of a claim, please:

  1. Contact our 24-hour claims helpline: 1800 778 877
  2. Report any theft or malicious damage to the Gardaí within 24 hours
  3. Take reasonable steps to minimise further loss or damage
  4. Keep damaged items for our inspection
  5. Provide all documentation and evidence we reasonably require
