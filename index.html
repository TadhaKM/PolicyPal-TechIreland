What is PolicyPal?

Your AI-powered insurance policy companion that makes complex documents simple

PolicyPal is an intelligent insurance analysis platform that uses advanced AI to decode your policy documents. We extract the essential information, highlight potential coverage gaps, and cross-reference your policy against real-world risk data - all in under 90 seconds.

Our hybrid intelligence approach combines the power of AI with deterministic rules to provide you with practical, actionable guidance - not just summaries.

Everything you need to know about PolicyPal

PolicyPal offers several key benefits:

  • Time Savings: Get comprehensive policy analysis in under 90 seconds instead of hours of manual reading
  • Clarity: Complex insurance jargon translated into plain English
  • Risk Awareness: Identify coverage gaps before they become costly claim rejections
  • Informed Decisions: Know exactly what questions to ask your broker
  • Peace of Mind: Understand what you're actually paying for

Risk Radar is our advanced feature that cross-references your policy coverage against real-world risk data:

  • Flood Risk: We check your property location against government flood maps
  • Crime Statistics: Local crime data helps identify if your contents coverage is adequate
  • Coastal Erosion: For coastal properties, we check erosion risk data
  • Subsidence Risk: Ground stability data for relevant areas

If your policy excludes something that's a real risk for your property, we'll flag it immediately.

Security and privacy are our top priorities:

  • Encryption: All documents are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Stateless Processing: We don't store your original documents longer than necessary for analysis
  • No Sharing: Your data is never sold or shared with third parties
  • GDPR Compliant: Full compliance with European data protection regulations
  • Audit Logs: Complete transparency on how your data is processed

You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Using PolicyPal is simple:

  1. Upload: Click "Upload your PDF" and select your insurance policy document
  2. Wait: Our AI analyzes your policy in under 90 seconds
  3. Review: Explore your personalized dashboard showing coverage, exclusions, and recommendations
  4. Act: Use our generated action plan and questions for your broker

No signup is required for the free tier - just upload and get instant results.

Our Hybrid Intelligence approach combines the best of both worlds:

  • AI/LLM Component: Advanced language models (similar to GPT-4) extract and understand policy text, handling the nuances of insurance language
  • Rules Engine: Deterministic, explainable rules that produce consistent, auditable recommendations

Why hybrid? Pure AI can sometimes hallucinate or produce inconsistent results. Pure rules can't handle the variety of insurance document formats. By combining them:

  • AI handles extraction and understanding
  • Rules ensure every recommendation is traceable and consistent
  • Every output includes citations and evidence
  • Results are reproducible and auditable

This approach gives you the flexibility of AI with the reliability of traditional software.
